Held-dose interpolation experiment

All measurements at each interior dose are held out together. Each model sees the remaining doses for that same drug. Endpoints of the tested dose range are excluded to avoid evaluating extrapolation.

This comparison measures prediction of held dose means within six existing drug curves. It does not measure prediction for a new drug, donor, laboratory, or clinical outcome.

Evidence needed before a submission claim

The public workbook omits chip IDs, donor IDs, cycle IDs, and vehicle-control records. We cannot separate donor variability from technical variability or assess control quality. The model estimates a measured functional endpoint and flags limited evidence; it does not certify drug safety.
