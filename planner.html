How the methods work

The local SmolLM2-135M model selects an action class from the numerical audit and your request. Python checks eligibility and supplies source-backed action text. Model scores are not probabilities of scientific correctness. Rules are the default because the model has not met the evaluation gate.

Training labels describe a heuristic action policy, not expert-adjudicated or biological ground truth. The planner does not optimise dose selection, certify drug safety or remove influential observations.
