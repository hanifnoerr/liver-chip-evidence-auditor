The evidence behind the examples

Source workbooks, measurement counts and the experimental information available for this analysis.

Public human Liver-Chip study

The example cases use day-three normalised albumin from Ewart et al., Communications Medicine (2022). Supplementary Data 8 supplies the response readings; Supplementary Data 1 supplies dosing context. This release analyses a subset of the published study.

What this subset cannot establish

Chip, donor and run mapping and raw vehicle/reference-control measurements are unavailable in the response subset. The two readings at a dose cannot be assumed to represent independent biological repeats. Assay calibration and device QC records are not assessed by this release.

The source contains albumin, ALT and morphology, totalling 210 scalar readings. Only albumin enters the current analysis. Unresolved ALT units and missing pairing keys prevent joint endpoint modelling.

Source-record IDs identify workbook cells. They are not chip IDs and cannot create experimental independence.

All published values and missing readings are retained. Synthetic chip IDs are used only in the simulator and are never assigned to published observations.
